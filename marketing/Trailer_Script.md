## Cutdowns (reuse the same footage)

- **6s loop (ads / Reels hook):** just the 0-5s block — cold-open slide + the three JUMP/SLIDE/SLASH cuts. Loops seamlessly, reads in silence.
- **15s (App Store preview minimum-effort):** rows 1-5 (0-17s), end card. Drops the Net beat.
- **App Store preview note:** Apple requires portrait preview for a portrait app, 15-30s, real device footage. This 28s cut qualifies as-is.

## Direction notes (why it's built this way)
- **Start on the swing, not the splash.** The first 2 seconds decide retention on every feed. Your strongest, most legible image is a last-frame dodge — lead with it. The logo goes at the END, when they already want it.
- **One idea per cut.** Muted viewers read text; don't stack two lessons on one shot.
- **Don't fake difficulty.** Use a real near-miss, not a staged one — cultivation fans will smell a scripted dodge.
- **Color is your tutorial.** The amber/cyan/red telegraphs already teach the mechanic on screen; let them, and keep text to verbs.
