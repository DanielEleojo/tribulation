## Description (limit 4000 characters)

> The road of cultivation is long, and bitter, and it does not care whether you are ready.

**Tribulation** is an endless-runner pilgrimage up the cultivator's road. You run three lanes down a stone path while a coalition of the righteous hunts you — and they do not lay dead obstacles in your way. They *attack*. Every leap, slide, sidestep, and slash is a real martial technique answered in the only instant you're given.

Read the qi-line. Choose the response. Survive long enough to break through.

**THE DEFENSE IS THE GAME**
Each hazard telegraphs its plane with a colored qi-line, so you learn to *read* attacks instead of memorizing a track:
• Amber low sweep → leap over it
• Cyan high slash → slide beneath it
• Red lane strike → step to the safe lane
• Slash the disciple, the ward, the flying daggers — or be caught

Late on the road, attacks layer: a high slash over a lane volley, a charging disciple behind a ground sweep. Mastery is reading two planes at once.

**CULTIVATE AS YOU RUN**
Gather Spirit Qi and sever foes to climb the realms — Qi Condensation, Foundation Establishment, Golden Core, Nascent Soul, Spirit Severing, and at last Ascension. Each realm doesn't just raise a number; it *awakens a new verb*:
• Qi Leap — a second jump drawn from the air
• Cloud Tread — glide over the hazards below
• Sword-Flight (御剑) — ride a flying sword into the aerial lanes
• Heavenly Tribulation — when your span fills, the heavens test you: a gauntlet of lightning you must survive to ascend. Fail, and you suffer Qi Deviation — the realm holds, but the climb resets.

**THE HEAVENLY NET**
A suppression formation contracts from the edges of the world. Standing still is death. Every kill you land pushes it back. The road forces you forward.

**THE LONG ROAD**
This is not a weekend. Your major realm is saved forever; your layer-climb is earned anew each life. Ascension is a goal measured in weeks and months — sharpen your reading, bank lifetime Qi, and spend it in the Cultivation hall: Spirit Gathering, Talisman Mastery, Qi Sea, Iron Body Refining.

Every run rolls fresh Cultivation Trials. A daily Qi reward keeps the streak. Twelve achievements and a Cultivation Journal mark the distance you've fled.

**FAIR. PREMIUM. YOURS.**
• No ads, ever
• No in-app purchases — the whole road is in the box
• No data collected, no tracking — your run stays on your device
• One-thumb portrait controls, built for the commute

The sects are coming. The Net is closing. Walk the road.
